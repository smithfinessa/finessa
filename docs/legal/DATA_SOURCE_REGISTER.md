# Legal Data Source Register

| Source | Intended role | Coverage | Verification treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| CourtListener | Case-law discovery/research | U.S. federal/state case-law corpus | RESEARCH_LEAD until authority/status/current-treatment checks |
| LegiScan | Legislative activity | 50 states + Congress | Legislative research; do not treat a bill as codified law |
| GovInfo | Federal primary documents | U.S. Code, CFR, Federal Register, public laws, bills and federal publications | Prefer as federal primary-source evidence where applicable |
| Official state sources | Codified statutes/rules/instructions | Per-state adapters | Preferred primary source; adapter-specific verification |

Commercial/API terms, redistribution permissions, attribution, rate limits, and data retention restrictions must be reviewed and recorded before enabling each production provider.
