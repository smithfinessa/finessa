# Public Launch Gates

Finessa must remain honest about its legal coverage and legal role. Public launch is not allowed unless the following conditions are met.

## Required rules

- No public claim of complete nationwide legal coverage for all states and territories.
- Every jurisdiction must have a source record with an official source link.
- Framework-only jurisdictions must remain clearly labeled as framework or unverified.
- A jurisdiction must not be represented as authoritative until it has been reviewed and verified.
- The product must not imply that it is a substitute for a lawyer or law firm.
- Public deployment requires counsel review, privacy review, and security review.
- All public marketing language must follow the marketing claims policy and legal disclosure policy.

## Production gate

Before any public launch, the project must meet all of the following:

- jurisdiction registry is complete and documented
- source verification status is recorded for each jurisdiction
- public docs describe the current scope honestly
- tests covering registry coverage pass
- legal review has been completed
- deployment controls are reviewed and approved
