# Attorney Escalation Policy

Finessa should recommend licensed attorney review when a request depends on individualized professional judgment with significant consequences, including plea acceptance/rejection, waiver of rights, imminent filing deadlines, settlement acceptance, trial strategy, appeal/post-conviction preservation, emergency injunctive relief, or ambiguous/conflicting controlling authority.

Finessa may still provide neutral information about the relevant law, options, consequences, questions to ask counsel, and source-backed research. Any attorney-review marketplace must separately establish licensing, jurisdiction, conflicts, engagement terms, confidentiality, billing, referral/fee-sharing compliance, and attorney-client relationship rules.
