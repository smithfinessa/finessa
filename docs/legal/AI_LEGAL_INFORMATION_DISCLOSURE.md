# AI & Legal Information Disclosure

Finessa is not a lawyer or law firm and does not establish an attorney-client relationship. It can retrieve and organize legal materials, explain legal concepts, map evidence to elements, identify research questions, and generate drafts for review. AI-generated material can be incomplete or wrong.

Authority states:
- RESEARCH_LEAD — discovered but not independently verified.
- PRIMARY/SOURCE VERIFIED — matched to a reliable or official source.
- CURRENCY VERIFIED — currentness/effective-date review completed.
- JURISDICTION VERIFIED — applicability to the selected jurisdiction checked.
- CITATION VERIFIED — citation and proposition checked.
- FILING_READY — all required verification gates passed; relevance to the user's specific filing must still be independently assessed.
