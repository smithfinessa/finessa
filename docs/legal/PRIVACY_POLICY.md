# Finessa Privacy Policy — Launch Draft

Case workspaces can contain highly sensitive legal and personal data. Production Finessa must document what it collects, why it collects it, retention periods, deletion/export procedures, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, cookies/analytics, payment-provider data, security controls, breach response, and user rights applicable by jurisdiction.

Minimum launch controls: TLS in transit; encrypted production storage where supported; least-privilege access; secrets outside source control; audit logging for privileged operations; secure backups; documented retention/deletion; and incident-response procedures.

This repository does not represent this draft as legally sufficient for every U.S. state or non-U.S. user. Counsel/privacy review is required before launch.
