# Finessa Terms of Use — Launch Draft

**Status:** product draft for counsel review; not legal approval.

Finessa is legal technology providing legal information, research, case organization, evidence mapping, and self-help document assistance. Finessa is not a law firm, does not represent users, and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Users must verify controlling law, deadlines, court rules, citations, evidence, and filing requirements.

The service may identify potentially relevant issues, authorities, defenses, claims, and procedural vehicles. Such outputs are research assistance, not a guarantee that a legal argument is available, preserved, meritorious, timely, or strategically advisable. Outputs marked RESEARCH_LEAD are not filing-ready.

Users may not use Finessa to conceal, destroy, falsify, fabricate, or unlawfully alter evidence; impersonate attorneys or court personnel; submit knowingly false statements; evade lawful process; or access another person's private case workspace without authorization.

Subscription limits, payment terms, cancellation/refund rules, dispute-resolution terms, governing law, age/eligibility, consumer notices, warranty limitations, liability limitations, and jurisdiction-specific provisions must be finalized with qualified counsel before commercial launch.
